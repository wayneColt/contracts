# Pricing footnotes · voice-missed-booking
Written to be quoted verbatim.
1. **What "under $5 a location" rests on.** Transcription and grading cost about four dollars per location-month at roughly 1,400 recorded calls of three and a half minutes, at published edge inference prices. The price you pay is above that; the footnote is the floor, not the offer.
2. **What happens at the cap.** When a location exceeds its plan's monthly call allowance, grading pauses and a digest is sent; no call is ever partially graded and no grade is invented to fill the gap.
3. **What "live" means, in seconds.** A grade lands within 120 seconds of hangup (measured on a live line, 2026-09-08). Live is a separate SKU because the module cannot sleep while it waits for the world.
4. **What is retained and for how long.** Audio is fetched once, transcribed, and discarded; it is never stored. Transcripts are retained for the buyer's chosen window (default 30 days) with card-shaped digit runs redacted, then hard-deleted. Grades are retained as long as the account is open.
5. **What is explicitly not included.** No dollar values are estimated (revenue comes only from a value column the buyer supplies). Outbound calls are graded for coaching and excluded from KPIs. No second phone vendor is required; the module reads the recordings the buyer already has.
